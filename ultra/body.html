The brief

Helping people navigate the journey of building a home

UltraTech Cement is a well-established brand in India. This brief focused on a different opportunity: helping people understand the home-building process itself.

Building a home involves many stages and decisions. For people unfamiliar with construction, knowing what happens at each stage, and how one leads to the next, can feel complex.

The client wanted a digital experience that explains the seven stages of home building more clearly and helps people feel more confident as they move through them.

Key design decisions

The thinking behind the experience

Five decisions shaped the design. To protect the concept, I describe the direction and show only a few cropped glimpses.

01A guided, stage-based journey

Seven clear stages instead of one long block of content

The challenge

People needed a clearer way to understand a process with many stages.

The direction

I structured the experience around seven stages, so the journey has a clear progression and people can see where they are.

Why it matters

Breaking a complex process into stages makes it easier to follow, and shows where each piece fits in the bigger journey.

02Audiovisual explanations

Explaining construction with pictures and a voice, not text alone

The challenge

Construction topics are hard to explain through text alone, especially for people new to the terms and the order of work.

The direction

The brief called for an audiovisual experience. I explored how visual content and spoken guidance could work together to explain each part of the journey.

Why it matters

Seeing and hearing an explanation gives people more than one way in, which can make the information more approachable.

03Hindi and English audio guidance

Guidance in the language people are most comfortable with

The challenge

The experience had to provide spoken explanations in two languages.

The direction

Hindi and English audio guidance is part of the intended experience, so people can take in home-building information in their preferred language.

Why it matters

Language choice affects how comfortably people understand information. Guidance in their own language makes the experience more accessible.

04A mobile-first experience

Designed for the phone in someone’s hand

The challenge

The information had to stay understandable within a mobile website.

The direction

I designed for the mobile context, with attention to content hierarchy, readability, and how guidance relates to navigation.

Why it matters

A focused mobile experience lets people take in information in smaller, more manageable pieces.

05Building confidence through clarity

Clear guidance to reduce uncertainty

The challenge

The client wanted people to feel more confident as they learned about building a home.

The direction

The experience centres on guided explanations and structured progression, supporting that goal.

Why it matters

Clear guidance can reduce uncertainty when people meet an unfamiliar process. It helps them understand, but does not replace professional advice.

The challenge and the learning

Designing guidance for a complex real-world journey

The most interesting part was turning a broad client objective into an experience that explains a complex process clearly. The brief brought together four requirements: a seven-stage journey, audiovisual guidance, bilingual audio, and a mobile-first interface.

Rather than treating them as separate features, the challenge was making them work as one experience. It reinforced for me that the interface has to follow what people need to understand, and that language, content and interaction all shape how clear an experience feels.
