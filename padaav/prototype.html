Padaav

One design system, four brands. Book a stay, then switch the brand at any step: colour, type, corners, motion and voice all change, and the flow keeps going.

Try it: open a house → View room → Reserve → pay → confirmation. Switch brand midway.

Self-initiated concept project. Padaav and its houses are fictional. Built from the same tokens as the Figma library (35 components, 304 screens). Photography from Pexels (Beladiya Nikunj, Forest Hills at Tala, Bertelli Fotografia, Anand Raj, Navlakha, Michael D Beckwith, Mographe, Teju, Vindhya, Davison Joseph, Aksinfo7, RPNickson, Scenehaus, Saveurs Secretes, Sabina Kallari and others), used for a concept.
