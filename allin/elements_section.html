08 Brand elements

A sticker box for every big moment.

A logo isn’t a brand. The brand is the stuff that shows up when something happens: a stamp when the trip locks, a ribbon when you settle up, a number that rolls instead of just changing. I designed these as a kit, each with one job and one way of moving. Tap any tile to replay it.

How things move
Drop0.6–0.8 s · overshoot

Cards and photos fall in at a slight angle, bounce once, and settle tilted.

Slam0.42 s · 2.4× → 1×

Stamps and pills hit hard, rotating from −30° to −12°.

Kinetic type70 ms per word

Big headlines rise word by word, like a story card.

Hover5.5 s · ±6 px

Once landed, floating cards breathe gently so screens never feel frozen.

Rule 1

One job each. The stamp means “done”, the pill means “paid”, confetti means “celebrate”. They never swap.

Rule 2

One sticker per card, never on top of text. My first perks pass was cluttered, so I cut it to the seal and the pill.

Rule 3

Motion is optional. With reduced motion on, everything appears in place, and no moment depends on animation to make sense.
