Casagrand A homepage that helps families choose a home

Search, compare, trust and visit, all from the first page.

I redesigned Casagrand’s homepage as a pitch at BC Webwise, designing it in Figma and building the interactive prototype with Claude. The page is built around the questions a homebuyer has: where, how much, can I trust the builder, and how do I see the home?

The idea

Four questions, one page

01Where can I live?

Search and browse by city.

02What can I afford?

Prices up front and an EMI planner.

03Can I trust them?

Handover record, RERA and stories.

04How do I see it?

Book a visit, call or WhatsApp.

Design decisions

Six decisions behind the page

01

Search before anything else

The hero is a search: city or project, budget, home type and possession date, with a live count of matching projects. One headline, one task.

02

Start with a city

Five cities as a simple index. Choosing one shows how many projects it has, the starting price and what’s ready to move into.

03

Price and possession up front

Every project card shows the price, BHK and possession date. Filters, a heart to save and compare-up-to-three help families shortlist together.

04

Show the record, not just a claim

“Promised vs delivered” puts each project’s promised and actual handover dates side by side. Trust comes from something buyers can check.

05

Help people decide

An EMI planner that reads like a sentence, five steps to a first home, and a path for NRIs buying from abroad.

06

Make the next step easy

A short visit form, plus Call, WhatsApp and Ask always one tap away. Visitors who aren’t buying get their own doors: owners, NRIs, partners, land owners and job seekers.

How it evolved

From my first version to v2

  1. v1

    My first version set the look: Inter with italic serif accents, Casagrand gold, night skies.

  2. v2

    Redesigned the homepage around search, cities, projects, proof and visits.

  3. v2.1

    Easier-to-read hero, a cleaner city index, a project carousel, one screen per section.

  4. v2.2

    Cleaner project cards, visual guide cards and the “I’m here as…” section.

  5. v2.3

    Three hero scenes that crossfade, with text always on open sky.

  6. v2.4

    “Promised vs delivered” as photo cards with a promise-to-keys timeline.

Outcome

A homepage proposal, ready to click through

The result is my homepage design in Figma, and an interactive prototype of it for desktop and mobile that I built with Claude. This was a pitch: it hasn’t been approved, launched or measured.

Prices, possession dates, RERA numbers and handover figures in the prototype are placeholders for the design concept, not Casagrand’s real data.
